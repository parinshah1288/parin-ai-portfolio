## Introduction

Training a large language model involves preparing data, operating a distributed training system, and testing the model before release. This infographic traces the workflow and shows how data, compute, energy, and elapsed time shape its cost and outcomes.

- [View the infographic](../assets/llm-training-infographic.html)
- [Read the explanatory document](../assets/llm-training-commentary.html)

## Approach

The challenge was comparing training figures with different units and levels of disclosure. The visual pairs Meta’s published Llama 3.1 405B training figures with a clearly labeled energy calculation. A common dollar scale compares Stanford’s estimates for GPT-4 and Gemini Ultra. The explanatory document covers the classification decisions, design rationale, and lessons for evaluating distributed training systems.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Mapping an LLM workflow from dataset collection through deployment and monitoring
- Interpreting accelerator usage, power assumptions, and compute-cost estimates
- Designing an accessible workflow graphic and a proportional comparison chart in HTML and CSS
